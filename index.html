How to play

Goal: Terrorists win a round by detonating the bomb at site A or B or eliminating the Counter-Terrorists. Counter-Terrorists win by defusing, eliminating the Terrorists before a plant, or running out the clock.

Touch: left thumb moves (the joystick appears where you touch). Drag on the right to look. Hold the red button to fire, and keep dragging on it to aim while shooting. Tap a weapon on the right to switch: primary, pistol, knife, grenades. With a grenade out, fire throws it and the scope button lobs it underhand. Hold USE on a site to plant (3s) or at the bomb to defuse (10s, 5s with a kit).

Keyboard: WASD, mouse, click fire, right-click scope or lob, R reload, E plant or defuse, Space jump, Shift walk, 1 primary, 2 pistol, 3 knife, 4 grenades (press again to cycle), Q last weapon, B buy, Tab scores, Esc pause.

Friendly fire: bullets and grenades hurt teammates (half damage). Kill one and you lose your guns: knife only, no buying and no money for the next 2 rounds.
