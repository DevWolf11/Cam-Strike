5v5 · Bomb Defusal · Sandstone

How to play

Goal: first team to 8 rounds. Terrorists win a round by detonating the bomb at site A or B or eliminating all Counter-Terrorists. Counter-Terrorists win by defusing the bomb, eliminating the Terrorists before a plant, or running out the clock.

Touch: left thumb = move (joystick appears where you touch). Drag anywhere on the right = look. The big crosshair button fires and can also be dragged to aim while shooting. Hold USE on a site to plant (3s) or near the bomb to defuse (10s, 5s with kit).

Keyboard: WASD move, mouse look, click fire, right-click scope, R reload, E plant/defuse (hold), Space jump, Shift walk, 1/2/Q weapons, B buy, Tab scoreboard, Esc pause.

Tip: stop moving before you shoot. Running ruins accuracy, just like the real thing.
